Brand guide

How Wellyfish looks and sounds.

Wellyfish helps dance studios find wellness programs and prepare their applications. This guide documents the design system the app is built on, so new screens, decks and posts feel like the same product.

02 · Color

Color tells you what to do

The page stays calm: Foam background, white panels, Mist borders. The four ocean colors each carry one meaning across the whole app, so an owner can tell at a glance what's confirmed, what the AI drafted, and what still needs them. Click a swatch to copy its hex.

What each color means

These come straight from ProvenanceBadge, fitLabel and statusLabel. Use the same color for the same meaning everywhere, including decks and emails.

Lagoon: confirmed or done

Facts from the owner's confirmed profile, providers that look like a fit, submitted applications, completed steps.

05 · Illustration & motion

One jellyfish, gently drifting

The product uses illustration rather than photography. The hero is the jellyfish on a soft gradient halo, with a few small bubbles in the brand colors.

Do

Use the halo (inset circle, Halo gradient) behind the large jellyfish.

Do

Add two or three bubbles at most: thin outlines in Lagoon or Coral at 60%, or a filled dot in Ocean at 40%.

Do

Let the jellyfish drift: 10px up and down over 6s, ease-in-out, looping.

Don't

Animate anything else. Drift is the one orchestrated motion, and it stops for prefers-reduced-motion.

Motion

@keyframes drift { 0%, 100% { transform: translateY(0) } 50% { transform: translateY(-10px) } }
.drift { animation: drift 6s ease-in-out infinite; }
@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) { .drift { animation: none; } }

Beyond drift, only spinners (while work runs) and short color transitions on hover.

06 · Voice

Takes the thinking out of it

Our users are studio owners who teach between admin tasks. Every sentence should save them time, tell them what happens next, and make clear that they're in control. Tagline: There's plenty of fish in the sea.

Plain

Short sentences, everyday words. "Find providers," not "Explore opportunities."

Honest about certainty

Say "Potential fit" and "Needs verification." Never promise someone qualifies.

Owner in control

The owner reviews and submits. We prepare, and we never act for them.

Says what's next

Every status comes with the next step: "Answer the remaining questions."

Say

“Potential fit”

Not

“You qualify!”

Say

“AI-drafted — review”

Not

“Auto-completed ✓”

Say

“You review every answer before anything is shared.”

Not

“Our AI handles your applications end to end.”

Say

“Two questions need your input.”

Not

“Error: incomplete submission.”

Writing the name

Always Wellyfish: one word, capital W. Never "WellyFish," "Welly Fish" or "Wellyfish AI."
